# Stadtplan „Werkstattmeile“

## Konzept

"Werkstattmeile", final synthesis. The base is the winning "flow" plan: a compact city shaped like a plus sign on two axes.
- **East–west axis:** the 960-stud Werkstattmeile (Z 0) holds the 8 private workshops, 4 on each side. Roundabouts close both ends.
- **North–south spine (X 0):** Parkplatz-Chaos, then the Ankunftshalle, then the Stadtplatz, then a signalised crossing, then the Autohaus, then the Teststrecke.
- **Arrival:** the city EMPFANG (Ankunftshalle) is a real walk-in hall with the CitySpawn and a 64-stud clock tower. Each of its 4 doors frames one destination:
  - south: plaza, gear fountain, Autohaus glass rotunda;
  - west: Schrottplatz gate;
  - east: Tuning-Zentrum;
  - north: the parking-puzzle grid.
- **Plaza buildings:** Spielhalle, Meisterschule, Auktionshaus (now with a portico) and Credit-Center open straight onto the plaza, so no road has to be crossed.
- **Industrial districts:** the Schrottplatz and Tuning-Zentrum sit behind the workshop rows. Each is reached through an alley from the Meile.
- **Farbleitsystem:** coloured floor lines lead from the spawn medallion to every door.

**Grafted from "visual":**
- 12-sided glass Autohaus rotunda on the plaza axis, with the hero car on a turntable
- Zahnradbrunnen with Kandelaber and festoon lights
- sawtooth Presse-Halle with a 56-stud chimney
- 66-stud Magnetkran with a verified reach table and a crane-to-feeder-bunker story
- Auktionshaus portico, pediment and tiered seating
- owner-directory pylon
- 8 skyline towers
- concrete night Lighting settings and the Neon lamp switch

**Grafted from "tech":**
- non-coplanar level stack
- 45° corner chamfers, with the loop-sampling proof (car disc r5, 0 violations, 0 shared lanes)
- a child `Arrival` Attachment on every station
- the 6-step rotation-safe code recipe
- distance-based light LOD

**Judge findings fixed:**
- Übergabe-Halle now has 24 clear.
- Parkhaus is a single upper deck with 24 clear underneath and a locked stair tower.
- Waschstraße now has 24 clear.
- Plots moved 2 studs off the Meile sidewalk for corner margin.
- A Marktstraße lamp that stood in a chamfer was moved.
- Meile asphalt extended under the Kreisel mouth.

**Verified numbers:**
- 0 overlaps between plots (including the full 4-bay halls), roads and buildings.
- Every district is ≤ 580 studs (30.6 s) from spawn by Dijkstra walk.
- Plot arrivals are 255–566 studs from spawn.
- About 7,740 static parts (24 template cars) and 105 PointLights.

## 1. Conventions

### 1.1 Axes
- X is east, Z is south (the "front"), Y is up. All values are world studs.
- Yaw `CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(yaw), 0)`. CarTemplate noses point to local −Z, so:

  | yaw | nose points |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | north (−Z) |
  | 90 | west (−X) |
  | 180 | south (+Z) |
  | −90 | east (+X) |

- Plot pivot: `CFrame.new(x,0,z) * CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(rot), 0)`, with rot ∈ {0, 180}.
- Rot 180 maps plot-local (lx, lz) to world (px − lx, pz − lz).

### 1.2 Level stack (tech rule: overlapping parts never share a top Y)

| Surface | Top Y | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Grass base slab (whole city) | −1.10 | 1 Part 1320 × 4 × 1010 at (0,−3.10,35); X −660..660, Z −470..540; Grass (73,91,64) |
| Outdoor ground plates: district yards, lots, plot yards, forecourts for vehicles, park paths | −1.00 | 0.3 thick (−1.30..−1.00) |
| Kreisel disc | −0.98 | Cylinder Ø76 |

Node plates: at every road node, one asphalt plate covers carriageway plus sidewalks (e.g. 44 × 46 at K-West). Sidewalk corner pieces sit on top of it, so the node plate's top is hidden under them.

### 1.3 Building shell rules
- Exterior walls 0.8 thick, slate (31,43,55), Metal. Roof slab 1.0 thick, graphite (42,48,55). Parapet above the roof.
- "Clear height" is the underside of the roof or trusses. Enterable interiors have ≥ 24; halls have 28–32.
- Doors ≥ 10 wide × 12 high.
- Interior partitions ≤ 12 high, so the follow camera (zoom 30) never snags.
- Every station prompt has ≥ 10 × 10 free floor on its "player side".

### 1.5 Hierarchy and station contract
- Hierarchy: `Workspace.City{Ground, Roads, Lights, Districts.<Name>, Stations.<key>, Arrivals.<key>, Animated, CitySpawn, PlotSlots.Slot_N{Pylon, Vacant, Lot}}`.
- Every district is a Model, so it is ready for ModelStreaming later.
- **Station part:** `City.Stations.<key>`. Invisible anchored Part 1 × 1 × 1, CanCollide false.
  - Attributes: `MiniTab`, optional `Game` / `Soon`.
  - ProximityPrompt: ActionText "Öffnen", KeyCode E, HoldDuration 0.25, MaxActivationDistance 10, RequiresLineOfSight false.
  - Child **Attachment `Arrival`** placed at the player stand point (3.5 above the floor on the player side, 6 studs out), oriented to look at the anchor.
  - The server Triggered handler range-checks the player against `Arrival`.
  - City stations never go under `Werkstatt.Stations` or `YardActivities`.
- **Arrival parts:** `City.Arrivals.<key>`. Invisible, CanCollide false, 6-stud free floor around them. The Y given below is the floor top; teleport to Y + 3.5.
- **Collision:** everything Anchored. Decorative parts (foliage, bulbs, neon strips, markings, sign text plates, small props) have CanCollide, CanTouch and CanQuery false, and CastShadow false.
- **Cars:** static display cars are clones of `ServerStorage.CarTemplates.<body>` (Root at origin, nose −Z, wheels on Y 0), recoloured through Paint/Hood/Mirror like `CarFactory.Spawn`.
  - Place the Root on the floor top (0 inside, −1.0 on outdoor ground, 3 on stages).
  - A template car counts 135 parts. Static template cars in the city: **24**.
  - NPC traffic, puzzle cars and wrecks are client-side "lite" cars (about 30 parts, derived from the templates) and do not count.

## 13. Integration notes and risks
1. **Code.** `C.PlotSlots` holds §4.2 in slot order. Apply the rotation fixes in §4.4 before anything else; without them, rot-180 slots land players behind the desk.
2. **Vacant kits** go `Parent = nil` before `W.Create`, because gravel and plot yard both sit at −1.0.
3. **Keep free.** Plot-local X −53..−33 and −30..30 in front of each plot stay free of city props.
4. **Station binding.** City stations are bound by MiniService (a separate Triggered binding with the `Arrival` range check), never by `W.Create`.
5. **Traffic, signals and animation** are purely visual and client-side. There is no server cost.
6. **Elevated surfaces are never arrival targets**: the Aussichtsplattform, stages and the Parkhaus deck.
7. **Template cars** are clones that are never edited. PreviewCars stay untouched. Wheel hiding on the Zerlegeplatz car only touches the city clone.
8. **Worldgen** should import the numbers from `/tmp/claude-0/scratch_design_final/layout.py` and `check.py` (loops, lamps, slot table) instead of retyping them.
9. **Build-complexity hotspots** (from Judge B):
   - Kreisel tangential sidewalks: 14 parts per ring, computed as angle k·(360/16) skipping the 2 mouth segments.
   - The rotunda 12-gon: 5 faces + 2 infills.
   - Sawtooth wedges.
   - Everything else is axis-aligned boxes.
